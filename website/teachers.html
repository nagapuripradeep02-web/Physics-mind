About

Made with teachers, in classrooms.

Every simulation is shaped by professors teaching real students, reviewed state by state, before it ships. If it doesn’t survive a live classroom, it doesn’t go in.

The physics is accurate by construction: rendering engines compute each frame from the governing equations, in real time. Nothing is key-framed, nothing is approximated art — so what your students see is what the mathematics says.

And it follows the NCERT backbone — the physics doesn’t change by board, so the same simulation teaches CBSE, ICSE and state board classrooms alike. Your lesson stays yours; the simulation is the picture inside it.
