## 1. Atomic claim

This concept teaches that **sine and cosine ARE the vertical height and horizontal distance of a point moving round a circle of radius 1, and that laying those lengths out against the angle produces the sine and cosine waves** — and only that. It does not cover general graph transformations `a·sin(b(x−h))+k` (deferred to `graph_transformations`, ranked-list P1 #1 — **including amplitude scaling `r·sin θ`, cut from this concept in round 1: F2, founder decision, no live-radius primitive**), trigonometric identities beyond the two the picture itself proves (`sin(θ+2π)=sin θ`, `sin(π−θ)=sin θ`), triangle-based ratio work (assumed Class-10 knowledge), or θ as a function of time / angular velocity (physics, SHM).
