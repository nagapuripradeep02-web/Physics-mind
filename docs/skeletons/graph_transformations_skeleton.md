## 1. Atomic claim

This concept teaches that **four numbers reshape any graph in four predictable ways — k slides it vertically, h slides it horizontally, a stretches it vertically, b squeezes it horizontally — and that numbers OUTSIDE the function act on y in the direction they say, while numbers INSIDE act on x in the opposite direction.** It does not cover the derivative (P1 #2, `derivative_as_secant_limit`), trigonometric identities or the origin of the sine shape (`unit_circle_to_sine_wave`, shipped), negative b / reflection in the y-axis (deferred — §2 + FLAGS), or transformations of specific families beyond the one parent drawn (generality is carried by the f-notation on S7's surface, never by a second apparatus).

## 2. Parent-function decision (dispatch-required, argued; ENDORSED at Checkpoint A)

**Parent: f(x) = sin x, for all states. Not switchable in explore.**

- **The degeneracy audit is the decision.** For every power-law parent, horizontal stretch IS a vertical stretch — `(bx)² = b²x²`, `√(bx) = √b·√x`, `|bx| = b|x|` — so the b-state's one motion would be visually indistinguishable from the a-state's, on the concept whose PRIMARY aha is that inside and outside are different. For an exponential parent, `a·eˣ = e^(x+ln a)` — the a-state collapses into the h-state. **sin is the only standard parent whose horizontal actions (period, phase) cannot be produced by any vertical action.** Its ONE coincidence — `−sin x = sin(x−π)` at exactly a = −1 — is disambiguated on-screen by the tracked point P′, whose path in S4 is a vertical straight line (a h-shift would move it horizontally); declared in S4's row.
- Continuity: the fleet's only shipped mathematics concept is `unit_circle_to_sine_wave` — the student who has it knows exactly what this curve IS (advisory prerequisite, §9).
- Payoff: amplitude/period vocabulary feeds physics (waves, AC) — the widest cross-subject overlap (38f).
- **Parent switch in explore: considered and DECLINED — on the dilution argument alone.** A fifth control on the explore state dilutes the four numbers the concept is named after, and a second parent demonstrates nothing the f-notation surface does not assert. *(Round 0 also argued a parabola would sit "mostly off-screen"; Checkpoint A refuted that — D4 breaks are shipped and working @3033–3038, and a clipped parabola is the standard textbook picture. The false argument is withdrawn; the decline stands.)*
- **Negative b: deferred, declared, with the sharper reason from Checkpoint A adopted:** sin is odd, so `sin(−bx) = −sin(bx)` — reflection in the y-axis would be visually IDENTICAL to reflection in the x-axis, reintroducing the exact a↔b ambiguity this parent was chosen to avoid. The b slider is positive-only everywhere. **Destination: none exists on the ranked list — escalated to the founder (FLAGS) as a roster gap**, and the four affected curriculum tags are downgraded honestly (§11 i-3).
