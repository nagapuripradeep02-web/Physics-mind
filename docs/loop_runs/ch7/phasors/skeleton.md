## 1. Atomic claim

This concept teaches the rotating-vector representation of AC — a sinusoid is the vertical shadow of an arrow
rotating steadily at ω, so amplitude becomes the arrow's length, instantaneous value becomes its shadow, and the
chapter's three settled phase facts (R in step, L a quarter behind, C a quarter ahead) become three FROZEN angles
readable in one glance because all of a circuit's phasors ride one shared clock — and only that. It does not
re-teach the R/L/C mechanisms (settled in the three sealed siblings), does not add or combine phasors
(tip-to-tail addition is deferred to `series_lcr_circuit`), does not treat impedance or power factor (deferred to
`series_lcr_circuit` / `ac_power_factor`), and does not use complex numbers (not in syllabus scope).
