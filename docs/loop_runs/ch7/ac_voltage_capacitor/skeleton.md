## 1. Atomic claim

It does not cover the inductor's mirror behaviour (taught in `ac_voltage_inductor`, sealed), the rotating-vector formalism (deferred to `phasors`, next), capacitor DC charging transients / RC time constants (taught in `capacitance`, shipped), or combining C with R and L (deferred to `series_lcr_circuit`).
