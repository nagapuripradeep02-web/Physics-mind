## Teacher-gate questions
- **The nematode's spelling.** NCERT XII Ch.12 itself prints "*Meloidegyne incognitia*"; the valid name is
  *Meloidogyne incognita*, which is what these cards print (correctly, per the brief). But two cards tell the
  student that the NCERT/guide spelling is a mistake. A board teacher must rule whether a script carrying
  the textbook's own spelling is marked down — if not, the `common_mistakes` entries should be softened to
  "write the correct spelling" rather than "this is an error".
- **GEAC's expansion.** NCERT prints "Genetic Engineering **Approval** Committee" (what the card teaches);
  the body was officially renamed the Genetic Engineering **Appraisal** Committee in 2010. The card only
  forbids "Advisory" (genuinely wrong), so nothing is prohibited that is right — but does the board accept
  "Appraisal"?
- **cry gene notation.** The cards write "Cry I Ac / Cry II Ab / Cry I Ab" with spaces and a capital C;
  NCERT writes the genes *cryIAc*, *cryIIAb*, *cryIAb* (italic, lower case) and the proteins Cry. The cards
  do keep gene-vs-protein straight ("the proteins of CRY I Ac … control"). Which form does the board expect
  written out in a notebook that cannot italicise?
- ***Phytophthora* called a fungus** (`ts_ipe_b2_bia_transgenic_plant_benefits` s1_resistance and its memory
  tip). Indian school texts class it with the fungi; modern classification puts it in the oomycetes. Left
  as printed — confirm the board's wording.
- **Transgenic Basmati "resistant to both biotic and abiotic stresses"** (same card, s4_hybrid) is a
  guide-sourced claim; the published transgenic Basmati work is bacterial-blight/stem-borer (biotic)
  resistance. Confirm before it is taught as the abiotic-stress example.
- **"Super weeds … grow faster than the crop"** (`ts_ipe_b2_bia_gm_biosafety_issues` s4_pollution). The
  defining property is weedicide resistance, which the line also gives; "grow faster than the crop" is the
  guide's phrasing. Acceptable or trim?

## Notes
- **Every printed line and every boxed answer in the group is biologically correct except the one HARMFUL
  boxed line.** The Bt mechanism (s3_mechanism) reproduces NCERT's chain exactly and in order — ingestion →
  alkaline gut solubilises the crystal → protoxin activated → binds midgut epithelial cells → pores →
  swelling and lysis → death — with the correct three insect groups (lepidopterans, coleopterans, dipterans)
  and the correct reason the bacterium survives (the toxin is stored as an inactive protoxin). The RNAi
  cards reproduce the sense/anti-sense → dsRNA → silencing route with *Agrobacterium* as the vector and the
  right consequence (the parasite cannot survive; the plant is protected). GEAC's two objectives are
  NCERT's own words. Biopiracy, Borlaug/Swaminathan, Flavr Savr (delayed ripening), golden rice
  (beta-carotene, precursor of vitamin A, Taipei variety), male-sterile *Brassica napus*, Round-up Ready
  soyabean and PCR/ELISA principles all check out.
- **The group's defect signature is the cry-gene shorthand.** "Ac and Ab for cotton; Ab alone for corn"
  appears three times — once as a boxed final answer (HARMFUL), once as a memory tip, once inside an insider
  note — and each time it destroys the roman numeral that is the only thing separating the two Ab genes.
  Every fully-written occurrence of the gene names in the group is correct, so this is a shorthand defect,
  not a knowledge defect; fixing the three strings fixes it.
- **Second signature: unverifiable mark and examiner claims** in card-level prose — "the third mark is
  safe", "the step examiners look for", "the one an examiner most wants", "this is the strongest mark",
  "half a point". Seven such strings across five cards, while every `verification.note` in the group states
  that the mark split is an unconfirmed claim. Five are filed above; "three of the four marks are"
  (pest_resistant_plants) and "the techniques are half the mark" (early_disease_detection) I left alone
  because both agree with their own card's `mark_split`.
- Source-book corrections confirmed correct: *Meloidogyne incognita* for the guide's "Meloidegyne
  incognitia" (both cards), and the Bt-cotton opener rewritten from the guide's garbled "produces an
  insecticide bollworm" to "makes its own insecticidal protein, which kills the cotton bollworm".
- Structure passed everywhere: 11/11 mark sums, 11/11 `mark_split` label-to-step-label matches in order,
  11/11 `expected_time_min` (4 VSAQ / 8 SAQ), no 0-mark step naming a mark, no figures in this group, and
  every `question_text` asks exactly what its steps answer. Zero exact prose clones against the other 87
