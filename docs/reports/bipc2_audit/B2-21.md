## Teacher-gate questions
1. **BOD expansion.** NCERT Ch.10 prints "biochemical oxygen demand", and `biological_treatment` s2_bod calls "Biological Oxygen Demand" an outright mistake. That expansion is in wide use in Indian texts. If a Telangana examiner accepts it, the mistakes line should say "write biochemical" rather than call the alternative an error (correction, not prohibition).
2. **Clostridium butylicum vs C. butyricum.** NCERT prints *butylicum* and the card follows it; the internationally valid name is *Clostridium butyricum*. Which spelling does the board expect, and is the other accepted?
3. **The second antibiotic fungus.** NCERT Ch.10 names only *Penicillium notatum*. The card's second fungus, *Penicillium griseofulvum* → griseofulvin, comes from the guide (whose printed "Pencillin griseoflavus" the card correctly repaired) and is externally verified, but it is off-NCERT. Does the board expect this pair, or *P. chrysogenum* / *Cephalosporium acremonium*?
4. **Bt brinjal as an answer to "name two GM crops".** It is genuinely GM, but it is under moratorium and not approved for cultivation in India. Does the board want an approved example (Bt cotton, golden rice) instead?
5. **The 100–1000 litre figure** in `fermentors` s1_what is NCERT's, but from Ch.11 (bioreactors), not Ch.10. Confirm a Ch.11 figure is welcome inside a Ch.10 answer.
6. **Is naming two of the four diseases enough** in `antibiotics_in_medicine`? The insider note instructs "write all four".
7. **Every mark split in this group is 1M (names) + 1M (products/uses)** — the bank's own claim on all eight cards, and every VERIFICATION_NOTE says so. One Telangana IPE teacher can settle all eight at once.
