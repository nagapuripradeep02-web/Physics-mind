## Teacher-gate questions
- **Gene mutation = point mutation only?** `ts_ipe_b2_piv_chromosomal_and_gene_mutations` s3_gene defines a gene mutation as "a change in a SINGLE BASE PAIR" and its MISTAKE[0] states it "affects ONE base pair". NCERT also treats frame-shift mutations (insertion or deletion of base pairs) as gene-level. Does the board accept the narrow equation gene mutation = point mutation, and should the mistakes line be softened so a student who names a frame-shift mutation is not told they are wrong?
- **"Ratio 1 : 0"** (`ts_ipe_b2_piv_test_cross` s3_design, CASE 1). Machine-checked as correct in substance (WW × ww gives all Ww, all violet), but "1 : 0" is unusual notation for a phenotypic ratio. Does the board want "1 : 0", "all violet", or "100% violet"?
- **Pea's "SMALL NUMBER of chromosomes (7 pairs)"** as reason 7 on `ts_ipe_b2_piv_pea_plant_advantages`. The count is right (2n = 14), but chromosomes were not known to Mendel, so it is a retrospective advantage rather than one he selected for. Indian guides print it; does the Telangana board accept it in an answer to "advantages of selecting pea plant for experiment by Mendel"?
- **How many reasons at 4 marks** for that same question — four (one per mark row), six (the card's mistakes line), seven (the source book), or nine (what the card prints)?
- **The lentil (Lens culinaris) seed-coat spotted/dotted co-dominance example** alongside ABO on `ts_ipe_b2_piv_codominance`: is the lentil case on the Telangana Botany-II syllabus, and is the human ABO example accepted in a BOTANY paper?
- **Is the law of dominance examined as three statements, or as three statements plus a fourth "what it explains" point?** The HARMFUL row above is filed on NCERT's three; if the board's own material numbers four, the fix is to align the card's steps and mistakes lines to four rather than the insider note to three.
