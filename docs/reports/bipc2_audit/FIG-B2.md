## Notes

- **The two examiner findings are confirmed by the render.** Five pins are visible under the phage
  base plate, and the lac operon's first caption does read "the six genes of the operon, in order"
  over six boxes of which three are genes.
- **`F.check()` cannot see any of these.** It tests stroke length, label bounds and label↔label
  clearance only. There is **no label↔stroke test and no leader-endpoint test**, which is exactly
  why `ld_nodule` (tail inside its own label), `cl_lam` (through the starch granule) and the phage's
  three-collinear-lines-on-y=208 all shipped green. A cheap gate would be worth having: for every
  `F.leader`, assert the head is within 8 u of some non-leader stroke and the tail is ≥6 u outside
  every label's box.
- **Label widths are all fallbacks.** `answer-book/tools/zoology_wip/label_widths.json` holds 102
  entries, every one of them zoology; **not one Botany-II string is measured**, so `label_w` uses
  `len × 9.9` at `sm` size for all 17 figures. Real Kalam at 17 px runs ~7.4/char, so the fallback
  over-estimates by ~30 %. That direction is safe for bounds but it means the recorded "measured
  Kalam widths" discipline of §7 is not actually in force here. Running `measure.mjs` over the b2
  render and merging the result would tighten every fix above (and is required before the lac operon
  legend fix, which is only a `check()` false-fail at 620 wide).
- **Judged from the render, not re-derived:** the `cl_stroma` and `kl_succ` endpoints are borderline
  rather than clearly wrong — `cl_stroma` stops 3 u inside the inner membrane and `kl_succ`'s tip
  just touches the "S" of "Succinic (4C)". Both are listed as secondary notes, not as the primary
  defect on their row.
- **Things I could not judge from the PNG.** Colour is not used anywhere (single ink), so no
  colour-coding claim could be checked. The `pen:"pencil"` / `wipe` strokes (`bp_core`,
  `g_divider`) render as dashed grey; whether that reads as "behind"/"construction line" to a
  student is a taste call for the founder, not something the frame settles.
- **Reductions that are NOT defects,** recorded so a repairer does not "fix" them: the rDNA figure
  shows 4 of the answer's 8 processes (no PCR, no gel, no downstream) and is a summary, not a
  contradiction; the test-cross figure shows only CASE 2; the glycolysis figure names 9 enzymes
  because step 5 (triose phosphate isomerase) is carried by the `⇌` in the "G-3-P (3C) ⇌ DHAP (3C)"
  row rather than by its own arrow; the Krebs tally block (2 CO₂ / 3 NADH / 1 FADH₂ / 1 ATP) floats
  inside the ring deliberately as a per-turn tally — but it carries no "per turn" heading, and one
  would help, since the card's boxed line supplies it and the figure does not.
- **Correct against §7's own checklist, verified one by one:** two chloroplast membranes with grana
  as three stacks of five separate discs joined by double lamellae ✓; guard cells substantial and
