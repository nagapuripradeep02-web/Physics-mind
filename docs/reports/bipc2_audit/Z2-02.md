## Teacher-gate questions
- **The products of intestinal lipase.** The card ts_ipe_z2_da_bile_no_enzymes prints "Lipase converts the emulsified fats into fatty acids and glycerol" — the standard Indian-text answer. NCERT's own intestinal equation is "Fats --lipases--> Di- and Monoglycerides", with fatty acids and glycerol appearing in the ABSORPTION section. Both are in print; which form does the board want on a two-mark answer?
- **Silicosis and coal miners.** ts_ipe_z2_beg_occupational_disorders MISTAKE[0] on s2 says "Writing silicosis for coal miners. Silicosis belongs to quarry workers." Coal-mine dust does contain silica and coal workers do develop silicosis as well as coal workers' pneumoconiosis. Is the prohibition safe at IPE level, or should it read "coal dust gives black lung disease; silica dust gives silicosis"?
- **How many types of papillae?** The card teaches three (fungiform, filiform, circumvallate), which is the Intermediate convention; standard human anatomy names four, adding foliate. Will a fourth type be accepted, and is three the number the board expects?
- **The parotid's location word.** NCERT gives "the parotids (cheek)"; the card gives "below the pinnae, the ear lobes". Which wording is safe to print, and is "below the ear" accepted on its own?
- **Breathing rate for the tidal-volume calculation.** The card uses 12–16 breaths a minute, which is exactly NCERT's 6000–8000 mL per minute at 500 mL a breath. Some Indian texts print 12–18, which would give 360–540 L an hour. Is a rate outside 12–16 accepted if the working is shown?
- **Do questions that ask only "name the types" carry marks for locations?** ts_ipe_z2_da_tongue_papillae asks only for the names, yet the split gives half the marks to locations; ts_ipe_z2_da_chyme asks only "What is chyme?", yet half its marks go to how chyme is formed. A board teacher must confirm whether these two splits stand, since both notes tell a student that the literal answer to the printed question is incomplete.
