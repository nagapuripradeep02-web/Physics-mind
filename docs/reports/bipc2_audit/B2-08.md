## Teacher-gate questions
- **IBA: natural or synthetic?** NCERT lists IAA and IBA as auxins "isolated from plants" and NAA and 2,4-D as synthetic; several commercial guides (and horticultural usage, where IBA is manufactured) call IBA synthetic. The HARMFUL row above follows NCERT. A Telangana IPE teacher should confirm which the board's key follows before the card is repaired.
- **"Respiratory climactic" vs "climacteric".** `ethylene_processes` s2 prints "RESPIRATORY CLIMACTIC", which is NCERT's own printed wording; the standard plant-physiology term is "respiratory climacteric". Left as printed. Confirm the board accepts (or expects) the NCERT spelling.
- **The chilling example.** `seed_dormancy` s3 names *Polygonum* for the chilling requirement. It is a standard seed-physiology example but is not in the NCERT paragraph; confirm the board's expected example (apple / peach / *Polygonum*).
- **Parthenocarpy.** `auxin_applications` s2 writes "promote fruit growth ... in tomatoes" where NCERT writes "induce parthenocarpy, e.g. in tomatoes". Confirm whether the key wants the term.
- **Two gibberellin responses outside the NCERT paragraph** — "9. They break seed and bud dormancy" and "10. They induce flowering in long day plants" are true and standard but are not in NCERT's gibberellin list. Confirm they are accepted in the Telangana key.
- **Larkspur** alongside cotton and coriander for heterophylly (NCERT names all three) — confirm the key accepts it.
- All ten cards carry `verification.status: unverified` with `needs_teacher_verification: true`, and every `VERIFICATION_NOTE` says the two-book union check and the board back-test are structurally impossible (one source book, no Telangana Botany-II paper in the corpus). The four-way 1M mark splits are therefore claims, not facts, on every card in this group.
