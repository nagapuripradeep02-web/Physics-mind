## Teacher-gate questions
- **Six NCERT steps or eight card steps?** NCERT XII Ch.11 sets the process out in SIX sub-sections (isolation of DNA · cutting at specific locations · amplification by PCR · insertion into the host · obtaining the foreign gene product · downstream processing), with gel separation + elution folded into the cutting section and ligation described with the vectors. The card prints EIGHT equally weighted steps and its `mark_split` gives one mark to each. Only a Telangana IPE teacher can say whether the examiner marks eight components or six.
- **The fourth transformation route.** NCERT names 'disarmed pathogen' vectors alongside heat shock, micro-injection and biolistics. Does the board expect it written, or are the three the card prints the full expected answer? (Filed above only as a WEAK on the closed count "Three methods are used".)
- **Which list of cloning-vector properties?** `laq_rdna_tools` s5_vectors prints the four-property guide list — low molecular weight · a unique cleavage site · ability to replicate in the host · a selectable marker. NCERT's own list of the features that facilitate cloning is origin of replication (ori) · selectable marker · cloning sites · vectors for plants and animals. Both are defensible; which does the board mark for the 2M component?
- **"Molecular glue".** NCERT calls restriction enzymes "molecular scissors" (the bank has a whole card on it), but I could not find "molecular glue" for ligase in NCERT. It is standard in Indian guides and I have NOT filed it as a Rule-41 metaphor for that reason — a board teacher should confirm it is acceptable inside an answer.
- **Does the second mark exist where the question does not ask for it?** Three VSAQs in this group reserve half the marks for material the printed question does not request: `artificial_plasmids` asks only "Name any two…" yet gives 1M to "What the names mean"; `cloning_vectors` asks for "an example" yet its component is "Examples" (plural); `define_biotechnology` asks only "Define biotechnology" yet gives 1M to "What it produces". The teaching is sound in all three; only a real teacher can say whether the names alone take the full 2 marks. Not filed as findings — the mark split is the bank's own claim.
- **Duplicate appearance row.** `downstream_processing` records `ap_ipe 2019` twice. Are these the March and supplementary sittings of the same year, or a data duplication?
