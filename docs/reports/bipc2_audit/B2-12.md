## Teacher-gate questions
- **"1 : 0" as a phenotypic ratio** (ts_ipe_b2_piv_phenotypic_ratios, parts b and d). The card writes "Phenotypic ratio 1 : 0 (all dominant)". This is guide-book convention; NCERT would simply say all the offspring are dominant. A Telangana examiner should rule whether "1 : 0" is accepted, or whether "all dominant (100 %)" must be written.
- **"Point mutation is also called a gene mutation"** (ts_ipe_b2_piv_point_mutation_inheritance, s1_define). The gene-vs-chromosomal dichotomy is standard in Indian guides and is used consistently across this bank (see the sibling card ts_ipe_b2_piv_chromosomal_and_gene_mutations), but NCERT does not print the equivalence, and strictly a gene mutation is the wider class (frameshifts included). Recorded as a convention, not filed as an error.
- **Is "back cross" accepted** for "What is the cross between the F1 progeny and the homozygous recessive parent called?" It is a true description; "test cross" is the specific name. The examiner should say whether the wider term scores.
- **Mark splits throughout.** Every card in this group carries `needs_teacher_verification: true` and a note saying the split is a claim; no Telangana Botany-II paper is in the corpus, so the 1M + 1M split on all eight is unconfirmed.
