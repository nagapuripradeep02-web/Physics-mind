## Teacher-gate questions
- **Seminiferous tubules per lobule.** NCERT prints "one to three"; this card (from the guide-book) prints "two or three" and marks "one" as an error. Which does the TS board accept?
- **Prostatic secretion pH.** The card prints "Its slightly acid secretion activates the sperms and feeds them." Measured prostatic fluid is about pH 6.5 (slightly acidic), but Guyton and several Indian texts describe it as slightly alkaline because it neutralises the vas and vaginal acidity. Which wording does the board mark?
- **Prostate's share of semen.** The card gives seminal vesicles 60% and prostate "15 to 30%"; at the lower bound that leaves 25% unassigned, against the commoner Indian textbook pairing of 60–70% / 20–30%. Which pair should the bank print?
- **Parturition hormones (plural).** The question asks for hormones; the card names oxytocin only. Does the board also expect relaxin (and/or the fall in progesterone, or prostaglandins)?
- **Menstrual-cycle hormones.** The card names four (FSH, LH, oestrogen, progesterone), matching NCERT. Does the board also want GnRH from the hypothalamus in the list?
- **Gestation duration.** Does the board expect NCERT's "about 9 months", or the 38/40-week pair the card prints, or both?
- **The six-part frame of the female system.** The card lists ovaries, fallopian tubes, uterus, vagina, vulva and accessory glands (mammary glands filed under the sixth); NCERT names the cervix separately and puts the mammary glands alongside the six. Is the card's enumeration the one the board marks?
