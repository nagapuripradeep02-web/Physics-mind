## Teacher-gate questions
- **Four-item VSAQs worth 2 marks.** `cancer_cell_four_features` and `fish_by_products` each print four
  items against a two-row `mark_split`. Is the board's scheme ½ mark per item, or 1 mark per pair as
  the cards assume? The answer decides whether the withdrawn "Each is a tick" note was defensible, and
  it applies to every four-for-two VSAQ in the unit.
- **Normal components of an ECG.** Does the board expect the four-class list (waves · intervals ·
  segments · complexes), or is the classical "P wave, QRS complex, T wave" a full answer? I graded the
  insider note's "not three" as condemning a correct alternative on the strength of NCERT Class 11
  Ch. 18 and the bank's own `ts_ipe_z2_abio_ecg_waves_intervals`; a teacher should confirm.
- **What "ECG" expands to.** This card writes "Electrocardiography, ECG"; its sibling
  `ts_ipe_z2_abio_ecg_waves_intervals` writes "ECG stands for electrocardiograph"; NCERT uses ECG for
  the graphical record. The bank should settle on one expansion across the unit.
- **`Catla catla`.** The board's material and this card use *Catla catla*; the currently accepted name
  is *Labeo catla* (also cited as *Gibelion catla*). Not filed as an error — confirm the board still
  wants *Catla catla*.
- **Fishery definition.** Is "or other aquatic animals" required for the second mark, or is "fish and
  shell fish" accepted in full? My WRONG finding rests only on the card contradicting itself, not on
  the board's scheme.
- **"ovaprim".** Printed lower-case; it is a trade name (Ovaprim), and some centres teach
  Ovaprim/Ovatide as *synthetic* hormone as opposed to pituitary extract. Confirm the capitalisation
  and that the synthetic option is accepted alongside the extract.

## Notes
- **The pattern in this group is the mark claim.** Seven of the eleven cards assert a mark scheme in
  prose ("the use is what earns the mark", "all three are marked", "The reason is the second mark",
  "Each is a tick", "It is the marked detail", "is a marked point", "cover the two marks"). Four of
  those assertions disagree with the card's own `mark_split` and are filed above; the other three
  (`hypophysation` insider "a marked point", `p53` s2 "the marked detail", `dairying` s1 "cover the
  two marks") happen to agree with the split and are left alone. A single sweep converting these to
  completeness language ("this is the point the answer needs") would clear the class.
- **Verified correct, do not "fix" back.** Direct ELISA → antigen and indirect ELISA → antibody (both
  the printed lines and both WHY explanations of which reagent is supplied are right). p53 arrests at
  **G₁**, and s2 MISTAKE[0] correctly says p53 does not repair the DNA itself. Insulin 51 = 21 (A) +
  30 (B) in two chains, machine-checked. `Catla catla`, `Labeo rohita` spelled exactly. Isinglass is
  from the air/swim bladder and does clarify wine; fish guano is a fertiliser from waste fish; shark
  and cod liver oils and omega-3 from sardines and salmon are all genuine by-products. Inbreeding →
  homozygosity → pure line, and accumulation of superior genes, is NCERT Ch. 9 verbatim in substance.
  Hypophysation = induced breeding, and s1 MISTAKE[0] correctly separates it from *hypophysis*, the
  pituitary itself. Contact inhibition, metastasis and apoptosis are all stated correctly on the
  cancer card.
- **Structure passed on all 11**: `marks_total` 2 = `mark_split` sum = step-marks sum; `mark_split`
  labels identical and in order to the step `mark_note`s; every card VSAQ with `expected_time_min` 4
  (group convention held); every `question_text` parses and asks what its steps answer, including the
  two-part ECG and insulin questions, whose second clause is answered explicitly. No figures.
- **Bank-wide duplicate-prose scan (all 3330 cards)** found only three shared strings, none harmful
  here: "Leaving out apoptosis." (shared with the 4-mark sibling `cancer_cell_features`, true in
  both), "Giving only one of the two." (shared with `primary_vs_secondary_antibodies`, true in both,

  (`dairying_factors` s2, `indian_and_exotic_carps` s2 and `oev_panspermia` s2_reached, accurate in
  all three). No cloned prose naming a structure, organism, hormone or number this group does not
