hold. The synovial-joint fixes are geometrically exact — `ld_outer` → 218 lands on `capsule_outer_l`
(217.5 at y=282), `ld_inner` → 222 lands on `capsule_inner_l` (222 at y=272), and `ld_ligament` → (200,187)
lands on the ligament (200 at y=187). The colour-blind routing fix is exact and preserves the
sperm-left / ovum-right endpoint convention that children 1 and 4 already follow, so no line needs to
be re-paired. The transgenic-animal and hypophysation text defects were not looked at.

**The 22-unit stacking bug is one bug, in four cards.** Every "rendered label collision" the sweep
found is the same thing: a two- or three-line label whose lines are spaced at exactly the font size
(22 u for a 22 px label, 22 u for a 17 px one), so the rendered `<text>` boxes — whose em box runs
about 1.2× the size in Kalam — overlap by a few pixels. It appears in `exc_nephron` (`lb_pct`/`lb_pct2`,
`lb_dct`/`lb_dct2`), `ncc_spinal_cord_ts` (`lb_ganglion`/`lb_ganglion2`, the reported 58×5 px), and
`gen_colour_blind_cross` (the four child columns and both parents). **A fifth card has it and the sweep
did not report it**: `hrs_female_system` and `bfc_heart_internal` stack their right-hand columns at 38 u,
which is safe, but any figure that ever wraps a label onto two lines at 22 u will fail. Suggest a
minimum line spacing of **28 u** as a `figlib` rule rather than fixing these four one at a time.

**`figlib.check()`'s out-of-bounds warnings are useless on Z2.** `label_widths.json` holds 102 measured
strings, all from Zoology-I; **not one Zoology-II string is in it**, so every Z2 label falls back to the
9.9 u/char worst case and `check()` reports eight labels "out of bounds" that the render shows sitting
comfortably inside the canvas (`lb_chordae`, `lb_papillary`, `lb_columnae`, `lb_pulm_veins`, `lb_crown`,
`lb_pct2`, `lb_dct2`, `lb_collecting`, `lb_canal`, `lb_bladder`, `lb_vesicle`, `lb_foramen`). Measured off
the renders, Kalam here runs **≈6.75 u/char at 17 px and ≈9.0 u/char at 22 px**. Nothing is actually
clipped — but `lb_foramen` "obturator foramen" ends at ≈510 of 520 and is the tightest label in the set,
so any repair that lengthens a right-column label needs measuring first. **Whoever repairs these should
run `measure.mjs` over the Z2 strings and merge them into `label_widths.json` additively.**

**Leaders that cross a labelled structure.** Distinct from a leader that *ends* on the wrong structure,
and not always fixable: `ld_sacrum`/`ld_coccyx`/`ld_symphysis` (pelvic girdle), `ld_vein`/`ld_vena`
(urinary system), `ld_artery` (through the whole right kidney), `ld_nerve` (tooth, through the blood
vessel 9 u before the nerve). Where the target is medial and the label lateral there is no route that
does not cross — `ld_vein` is the clearest case, since the renal vein is medial to the kidney and the
label is on the far left. Only `ld_aorta` was flagged as a required fix, because it crosses the inferior
vena cava, which carries its own label 10 units away, and there **is** a clean route (the empty band
above y=24).

**What I could not judge from the PNGs.** (1) Whether any label is truly clipped — the gallery renders
each figure inside a dashed box at ~0.8 scale, so a label ending within ~10 u of the edge cannot be
called; `lb_foramen` and `lb_crown` are both in that band. (2) The exact rendered overlap of the stacked
labels — I inferred the em-box cause from the reported 58×5 px on the spinal cord and the fact that
every reported collision is a 22 u stack; the repairer should confirm with
`node answer-book/tools/find_label_clashes.mjs` after re-spacing. (3) Animation and pacing — the shots
are cumulative stills, so phase *order* is visible but nothing about `ms` or the tap-through feel.
Every phase order I could see is the order a teacher draws: outline → internal structures group by
group → leaders and labels last, with no phase caption describing something it does not draw.

**Two proposed fixes change a silhouette and must be re-rendered before being believed** — the tooth's
crown/neck (it moves the neck endpoints, which six other strokes anchor to) and the spinal cord's grey
matter (a full replacement of both lobes plus the commissure). The heart's semilunar valves sit in a
42 u gap between the tricuspid and bicuspid cusps and may not fit; if they do not, the honest fallback
is to extend the two great-vessel roots to the ventricles without drawing the valves, and record the
omission on the card rather than leave the roots at atrial level.
