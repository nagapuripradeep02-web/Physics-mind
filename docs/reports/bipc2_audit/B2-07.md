## Teacher-gate questions
- **Krebs step 7 — ATP or GTP?** NCERT XI Ch.14 writes the succinyl-CoA step as producing GTP, which is then converted to ATP in a coupled reaction; the card writes "Succinyl CoA + ADP + Pi --thiokinase--> succinic acid + ATP + CoA" directly. The per-turn tally of 1 ATP is unaffected either way. Which form does the Telangana board expect in the printed equation?
- **Phase names in glycolysis.** The card uses "Energy acquiring phase" (steps 1-5) and "Energy releasing phase" (steps 6-10); other texts use "energy investment / preparatory phase" and "energy payoff phase". Is the card's pair the accepted wording? (The s4_phase1 WHY explains the name as the sugar acquiring energy, which is coherent, but a teacher should confirm the label.)
- **Enzyme-name dialect.** phosphoglycerokinase vs phosphoglycerate kinase · phosphohexose isomerase vs phosphoglucose isomerase · citrate synthase vs citrate synthetase or "condensing enzyme" · the bare "dehydrogenase" printed at Krebs steps 4, 8 and 10 vs the full isocitrate / succinate / malate dehydrogenase. All are correct chemistry; which spellings does the board accept?
- **The fat used for the RQ example.** The card uses triolein (57/80 = 0.7125, printed as 0.7); NCERT uses tripalmitin (102/145 = 0.7034, also printed as 0.7). Both are standard in Indian texts. Is triolein accepted, and should the printed value be 0.7 or 0.71?
- **Are the diagrams really unmarked?** Both LAQ cards assert "No diagram mark" (0M steps) on 8-mark questions, and both VERIFICATION_NOTEs say the mark split is a claim until a teacher confirms it. Many examiners award a mark for the glycolysis flow chart and the Krebs ring — this needs ruling on before the 0M is trusted.
- **Ten-step Krebs vs the NCERT simplified cycle.** The written answer uses the ten-step version including cis-aconitic acid and oxalosuccinic acid; the figure shows only eight compounds. Is the ten-step form expected, and is an eight-compound ring acceptable alongside it?
- **Krebs step 1 with water on the left.** The card prints "Acetyl CoA + oxaloacetic acid + H2O --citrate synthase--> citric acid + CoA"; some texts omit the H2O. Either accepted?
