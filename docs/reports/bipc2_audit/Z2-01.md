## Teacher-gate questions
- **The 7 per cent route.** NCERT Ch.17 says "7 per cent of CO₂ is carried in a **dissolved state** through plasma"; `co2_transport` writes it as "About 7% as carbonic acid" in the list, the heading and the step lines, while that step's own `why` describes it as gas that "simply dissolves". Both wordings are in circulation in Indian texts. Which does the board mark, and should s2 read "carried dissolved in the plasma, where a little of it forms carbonic acid"?
- **COPD scope.** Does the Telangana answer include asthma under COPD? The medical definition does not, and the card's own s2 applies the term to emphysema alone — this decides the exact wording of the HARMFUL row above, though asthma is wrong under either reading.
- **Where fat digestion starts.** NCERT records small amounts of lipases secreted by the gastric glands. Does the board accept "a little fat digestion begins in the stomach", or does it want the flat "fat is digested only in the small intestine" the card prints? The wording of the butter card's WRONG row depends on the answer.
- **Oxygen and the aortic/carotid receptors.** NCERT: "The role of oxygen in the regulation of respiratory rhythm is quite insignificant." Physiologically the aortic and carotid bodies ARE the oxygen sensors. Does the board want oxygen named in this 4-mark answer? If yes it belongs in the printed lines of s4, not in a `common_mistakes` entry that contradicts them.
- **Axes of thoracic expansion.** NCERT gives the diaphragm → antero-posterior axis and the external intercostals → dorso-ventral axis. `inspiration_expiration` names only the dorso-ventral axis and omits the antero-posterior one for the diaphragm. Does the board require both axes named for the two volume marks?
- **Tooth diagram parts.** Should cement (cementum) and root canal be labelled in the board's expected L.S. of a tooth? The card labels eight parts and labels neither, although it draws the canal. And what crown : root proportion does the board expect — the card claims "about twice" and draws 1.5 times?
- **Conchae.** Three per side or three in total, and is the expected function "regulate the temperature" alone or the fuller "warm, moisten and filter the inhaled air"?
- **Rennin in gastric juice.** `protein_digestion_stomach` lists rennin among the general contents of gastric juice and then confines it to infants (NCERT: "found in gastric juice of infants"). Is the general listing accepted?
