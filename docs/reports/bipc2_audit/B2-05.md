## Teacher-gate questions
- **Transpiration row (`c3_vs_c4` s3_photoresp, row 6).** The card prints the bare comparison "Transpiration is MORE / LESS". The physiologically precise quantity is the **transpiration ratio** (water transpired per unit dry matter: roughly 450–950 in C3 against 250–350 in C4); absolute transpiration depends on conditions. Indian guide tables print both forms. Does the Telangana scheme accept the bare row, or does it want "transpiration ratio"? Not filed as an error — it is a convention difference.
- **C3 climate row (row 2).** "Occurs mostly in TEMPERATE plants" is the standard guide-table row, but C3 is the majority pathway in every climate, not a temperate-only one. Confirm the board accepts the temperate/tropical pairing as printed.
- **Eight versus nine rows (`c3_vs_c4`).** The question says "any eight" and the card supplies nine. A teacher should confirm whether the board's scheme expects a fixed set of eight (and whether the examples row is one of them), which would settle the two findings filed against `s4_yield` and `s2_anatomy`.
- **`one ATP for phosphorylation` (`calvin_cycle` s4).** That sentence is NCERT's own per-RuBP wording. Confirm the board accepts it as printed, or expects the six-turn totals (18 ATP, 12 NADPH per glucose) spelled out, before the fix above is applied.
- **Phase name (`calvin_cycle`).** The printed line says "1. CARBON FIXATION" while the figure labels the same arc "Carboxylation". NCERT uses both. Confirm either is accepted so the card can settle on one.
- **Calvin cycle at 4 marks.** The `VERIFICATION_NOTE` already flags this: the book prints two pages and a full cycle diagram, the LAQ chapter cross-references it, but the 2022 blue print gives Photosynthesis 6 marks with no Section-C slot. A Telangana teacher should confirm there is genuinely no 8-mark form of this question before the 4-mark authoring is locked.
- **Diagram-only cards.** `chloroplast_diagram` carries all four marks on one drawing step and has no `boxed_final` line at all — the only card in the group without one. Confirm the house rule for "draw a neat labelled diagram" questions (see the mark_split finding above).
