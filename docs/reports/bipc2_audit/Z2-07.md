## Teacher-gate questions
1. **Pituitary — two parts or three?** NCERT divides it anatomically into adenohypophysis and neurohypophysis (with pars distalis + pars intermedia inside the adenohypophysis) and adds that in humans the pars intermedia is almost merged with the pars distalis. The card lists three parts. Does the TS key accept a two-part answer? (This is the basis of the first WRONG row.)
2. **Congenital hypothyroidism** — NCERT's name is **cretinism**; `ts_ipe_z2_end_hypo_hyperthyroidism` s2 writes "thyroid dwarfism" with NCERT's exact symptom list. Is cretinism required, accepted alongside, or is thyroid dwarfism the board's own term?
3. **The sixth anterior hormone** — NCERT names prolactin (PRL); the card writes "Luteotropic hormone, LTH" and adds "maintains the corpus luteum". Which name does the key use, and is the corpus-luteum function expected in a TS script?
4. **Lipid-soluble hormone receptors** — NCERT says intracellular receptors, "mostly nuclear receptors"; `ts_ipe_z2_end_hormone_action_mechanism` s4 says "receptors in the cytoplasm". Is cytoplasm-only accepted, or should the line read "inside the cell (cytoplasm or nucleus)"?
5. **Hypothalamic examples** — NCERT's named pair is GnRH (releasing) and somatostatin (inhibiting); the card uses somatocrinin/somatostatin, which is the better teaching pair because both act on GH. Is "somatocrinin" accepted on a TS script, or should GHRH be given alongside?
6. **HIV latent period** — the card prints "a few months to 10 years"; NCERT says "a few months to many years (usually 5-10 years)". Which figure does the key expect?
7. **Mark-scheme phrasing across the group.** Every verification note states the mark split is the bank's own claim, yet nine prose fields assert mark behaviour: "that single word carries the mark", "what the examiner ticks", "Each one is a tick", "Both halves are marked equally", "Inhibiting hormones are equally marked", "Both sites are marked", "the function is what is marked, not the name alone", "Leydig cells is the marked answer", "earns the structure mark on its own". The two self-contradicting ones are filed as WRONG above; a teacher should confirm or soften the rest.
