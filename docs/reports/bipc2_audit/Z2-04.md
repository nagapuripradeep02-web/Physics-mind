## Teacher-gate questions
- **Columns of Bertin and filtration.** `s2_function` `common_mistakes[1]` says "Writing that they filter blood. They do not." The columns are cortical tissue and therefore contain cortical nephron material as well as the interlobar vessels, so the flat denial may be too strong. Does the board want the softer "their job is to separate the pyramids, not to filter"?
- **Cortical nephron vasa recta.** NCERT: "absent or highly reduced". Does the Telangana board accept "reduced", and does it accept the flat "absent" the card prints? (Filed above as WEAK, not WRONG, because both readings are in circulation.)
- **Rennin's substrate chain.** The card boxes "Rennin converts casein into calcium paracaseinate" (the state-guide form). Some texts write caseinogen → casein → calcium paracaseinate. Which chain does the board mark, and must "gastric glands of infants" be named for the source mark?
- **GFR value.** Is "about 180 litres per day" accepted on its own for the value mark, or must "125 mL per minute" appear? The card's mistakes line and its memory tip pull in opposite directions on this.
- **Mark splits are the bank's own reading** on all eight cards (each `verification.note` says so), yet three insider notes (`columns_of_bertin`, `nephron_units_types`, `glomerular_filtration`) speak about what the board marks. A teacher should confirm the eight splits before those notes stand.
- **"Superficial renal cortex"** (cortical nephrons) — is the board's expected wording "outer cortex" / "renal cortex"?
