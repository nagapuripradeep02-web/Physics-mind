## Teacher-gate questions
- **Sickle-cell codon notation.** NCERT XII Ch.5 prints the change as "GAG to **GUG**" (mRNA), while `ts_ipe_b2_mbi_point_mutation_molecular` prints "In the **gene** ... GAG becomes **GTG**" (DNA). Both are internally correct — the card's is the more precise of the two — but a board teacher should rule on which the examiner expects, since the boxed answer is the GTG form.
- **Scope of "point mutation".** The same card's s1 line says a point mutation "may be a substitution, an insertion or a deletion of one base". Standard genetics agrees; NCERT XII Ch.5 introduces insertions and deletions as *frame-shift* mutations in the same paragraph as its point-mutation definition. Does the board accept the three-kinds phrasing?
- **Does the second mark exist where the question does not ask for it?** `ts_ipe_b2_mbi_rna_viruses` asks only "Name any three viruses..." yet the card's `mark_split` reserves 1M for "What that shows" and `MISTAKE[1]` calls omitting it a mistake; `ts_ipe_b2_mbi_who_proved_dna` asks for "the organism" (singular) yet its `MISTAKE[1]` requires the phage AND the host. Both are sound teaching; only a real teacher can say whether three names alone, or "bacteriophage T2" alone, takes the full 2 marks. Not filed as findings — the mark split is the bank's own claim.
- **Duplicate appearance rows.** `point_mutation_molecular` records `ts_ipe 2019` twice and `transcription_unit` records `ts_ipe 2017` twice, with no field distinguishing them. Are these the March and supplementary sessions of the same year, or a data duplication? 12 of the 167 Botany-II cards have this shape.
