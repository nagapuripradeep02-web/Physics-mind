## Teacher-gate questions
- **Karyotype convention.** The group uses both conventions and condemns one of them: `sex_determination_humans` prints 44XX / 44XY / 22X and calls 46XX and 23X mistakes, while `laq_genetic_disorders` prints 47,XXY and 45,X, which is the all-chromosome convention under which a normal female IS 46,XX. Which does the TS board expect, and does it accept the other? (Note also that 44XX is written without the "+" that the autosome convention needs: 44 + XX.)
- **Colour-blindness allele symbols.** Both crisscross cards use Xᶜ for the NORMAL allele and Xᶜᵇ for the colour-blind allele. Several Indian texts use the opposite sense of the small "c" (Xᶜ = colour blind, Xᶜ capital = normal). Which does the board accept, and should the cards print a key line?
- **Turner's karyotype** is printed 45,X; 45,X0 is equally current. Confirm which the board wants.
- **The third ABO allele** is printed as "Iᴬ, Iᴮ and Iᴼ (or i)" on both ABO cards. Confirm the board accepts Iᴼ, since NCERT uses i.
- **Anti-D timing.** NCERT says the injection is given immediately after the delivery of the first child; modern obstetric practice also gives it antenatally at about 28 weeks. Confirm the board marks the NCERT timing (this is the basis of the HARMFUL row above).
- **Is CML a "common genetic disorder"?** The Philadelphia translocation is an acquired somatic change, not an inherited one, as the card's own WHY concedes ("a cancer rather than a syndrome present from birth"). Confirm the board expects it inside this essay.
- **n(n+1)/2** is printed as the required working for the ABO genotype count. Confirm the board expects the formula rather than the plain list of six genotypes.
