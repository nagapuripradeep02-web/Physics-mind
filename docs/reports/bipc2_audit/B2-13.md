## Teacher-gate questions
- **146 bp against NCERT's 200 bp** (ts_ipe_b2_mbi_nucleosome). NCERT Class 12 Ch.6 prints "A typical nucleosome contains 200 bp of DNA helix"; the guide printed "about 150 base pairs"; the card prints 146 bp wrapped in ~1.75 turns, which is the correct crystallographic figure and the bank's decided position. All three numbers are in circulation. A Telangana examiner must rule whether 146 bp alone is accepted, or whether the ~200 bp repeat must appear on the page. (The card should carry both either way — see the WRONG row.)
- **RNA size range "about 75 to 2000 nucleotides"** (ts_ipe_b2_mbi_dna_vs_rna, s2_stability, row 6). Guide-book convention. The lower bound is right (tRNA ≈ 75), but 28S rRNA runs to roughly 5000 nucleotides, so 2000 is not a real ceiling. Does the board expect the printed range, or "much smaller than DNA — from about 75 nucleotides upward"? Recorded as a convention, not filed.
- **The lac operon gene map: i-p-o-z-y-a against the guide's p-i-p-o-z-y-a** (ts_ipe_b2_mbi_lac_operon_diagram, s1_diagram MISTAKE[1]). NCERT Fig. 6.14 draws i-p-o-z-y-a and the card follows it, correctly. But the guide's version is not an error — it shows the i gene's own promoter as well as the operon promoter, which is the real arrangement. MISTAKE[1] states the NCERT order as "the" order. A teacher should rule whether a student who draws the i gene's own promoter is marked down; if not, MISTAKE[1] wants softening to name only the ordering error it is really aimed at (o before p, or z-y-a before o).
- **"Three RNA polymerases in cells"** (ts_ipe_b2_mbi_rna_polymerases, s1_how_many). NCERT reads "at least three RNA polymerases in the nucleus (**in addition to the RNA polymerase found in the organelles**)". The card's first line is "In eukaryotic cells there are THREE types of RNA polymerase", which excludes the organellar enzyme — and this is a botany paper, where the chloroplast polymerase is on the syllabus elsewhere. Should the answer name the organellar polymerase, or is "three, all in the nucleus" the expected Telangana answer? Not filed as an error; the card's second line ("All three are found in the NUCLEUS") already carries the qualifier the question needs.
- **"DNA is of ONE type"** (ts_ipe_b2_mbi_dna_vs_rna, row 11). Beyond the garbled parenthetical filed as WEAK, the row itself needs a ruling: DNA has A, B and Z forms, so "one type" is a statement about metabolic classes, not structure. Confirm the wording the board expects opposite "THREE types: mRNA, tRNA, rRNA".
