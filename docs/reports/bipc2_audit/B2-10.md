## Teacher-gate questions
- **TMV nucleotide count.** Both TMV cards print "about 6,500 nucleotides". The measured TMV genome is
  6,395 nt and many Indian texts print 6,400. Recorded here as a convention, not a finding — a
  Telangana teacher should say which number the board accepts.
- **How many ICTV levels.** The card teaches THREE hierarchical levels (family, genus, species), which
  is what the state textbook prints. ICTV itself also uses the rank ORDER (-virales) and, since 2018,
  ranks above it. Confirm the board wants only the three, so the s2 MISTAKE lines can be worded to
  forbid only what is genuinely wrong.
- **How a species is named.** The card says a species name is "a common English expression". ICTV moved
  to binomial, Latinised species names in 2021. Confirm the board still wants the older
  English-descriptive rule and the species written as "Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)".
- **Is the envelope part of "the chemical structure of viruses"?** Core + capsid is the two-component
  answer the card gives. Confirm whether the board expects the lipid envelope of enveloped viruses
  (HIV, influenza) named as a third covering — this decides how far the s1 MISTAKE[0] prohibition may go.
- **Lysozyme outside the phage cycle.** Lysozyme is also a natural enzyme of the host (tears, saliva,
  egg white). Confirm that in the Viruses chapter the expected answer is the phage-coded enzyme only,
  so "coded by the VIRUS, not by the host" is read as the lytic-cycle enzyme and not as a general
  definition of lysozyme.
- **Burst size.** The card prints "About 100 to 200 phages" per bacterial cell; some texts give ~300
  for T4. Confirm the accepted range.
- **Mark splits.** All eight cards carry `needs_teacher_verification: true` and their splits are the
  bank's own claim; only internal consistency was auditable here, and it holds (see Notes).
