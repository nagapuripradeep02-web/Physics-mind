## Teacher-gate questions
- **Blackman's wording.** NCERT prints "the factor which is nearest to its minimal value"; Blackman's own 1905 sentence is "limited by the pace of the slowest factor"; the card prints the guide's "present in a RELATIVE MINIMAL VALUE". All three carry the same meaning — which wording does a TS IPE valuer expect, and is "relative minimal value" really the scanned phrase (see the WEAK on the insider note)?
- **Grana vs thylakoid.** Does the board accept "thylakoid membranes" (NCERT's own site for the light reaction and for photolysis) when the question names the grana? The answer decides whether the grana card's `MISTAKE[1]` is merely over-strict or must be deleted.
- **Unit placement of the phloem card.** Phloem transport and the ringing/girdling experiment are NCERT's *Transport in Plants*, not *Photosynthesis in Higher Plants*; the card sits in Unit 4 only because the guide grouped it on p.40 with the photosynthesis questions. Should it be re-filed?
- **Precision expected for the photolysis site.** Is "in the grana, during the light reaction" a full mark, or does the board want "on the inner side of the thylakoid membrane, associated with PS II"?
- **The 1M+1M split on a "Define" question.** Does a complete definition with no worked example score both marks on the limiting-factors question? The bank's own verification note calls the split a claim.
