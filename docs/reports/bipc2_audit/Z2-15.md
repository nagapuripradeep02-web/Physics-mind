## Teacher-gate questions
- **Broiler market age.** The card prints 8 to 10 weeks. Other Indian animal-husbandry texts print 6 to 8 weeks, and commercial practice is now 5 to 7 weeks. Which figure does the Telangana board accept in a 2-mark definition?
- **"3-D cross sectional picture" for a tomogram.** Indian Intermediate texts print exactly this, and the card's `common_mistakes[1]` defends it ("Saying it is a flat two dimensional X-ray"). In radiology a tomogram is a single 2-D slice and the 3-D picture is the reconstruction from many slices. Confirm the wording the board wants — the card is internally consistent either way, so this is a convention call, not a listed finding.
- **"Delay from SA node to AV node".** A prolonged P-R is first-degree AV block, i.e. the hold-up is in conduction THROUGH the AV node. Board texts phrase it as the card does (SA → AV). Confirm the accepted phrasing before anyone "corrects" it.
- **Does the full form of PCR earn a mark** in a question that asks for two features? This decides the `mark_split` relabel proposed above.
- **Is "exclusively" a required word** in the layer definition? `common_mistakes[1]` on `layer_and_broiler` s1 says leaving it out is an error, while the same card's insider note says the deciding word is "eggs". Not filed as a finding because "exclusively" is in the standard printed definition; a teacher should rule.
- **Normal P-R interval quoted as 0.12 to 0.2 s** — matches the sibling `ecg_waves_intervals` card exactly; recorded here only as the convention this bank uses, not as a defect.
