## Teacher-gate questions
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_mutation_theory`: does the Telangana textbook print "O. levifolia" or "O. laevifolia"? De Vries's published epithet is *laevifolia*; "levifolia" is common in Indian guides. Same question for "Small style form" vs "Short style form" for *O. brevistylis* (filed WEAK, not WRONG, because it may be the board's own spelling).
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_natural_selection_melanism`: NCERT calls the two forms "**white-winged**" and "dark-winged / melanised"; the card prints "**grey** and black". Both describe *Biston betularia*, but a student may write "white" from NCERT and "grey" from this card. Which does the key print? Should the binomial *Biston betularia* be added, given the card never gives a scientific name?
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_neo_darwinism`: the card lists chromosomal mutations as "**addition**, deletion, inversion and translocation". The standard structural set is deletion, **duplication**, inversion, translocation. Is "addition" the board text's word?
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_genetic_drift_founder_effect` s2_fixation: "The chance of reaching either end point depends on the size of the population." For a neutral allele the *probability* of fixation equals its current frequency whatever the population size; what population size governs is how *fast* drift reaches one of the two end points. The card's wording is defensible read as "how soon it ends", but confirm the board's sentence.
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_genetic_drift_founder_effect` s4: does the source name the population for the near-100% O blood group example (South American tribal populations is the usual one), and does it say "O" or "O⁺"?
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_genetic_load`: the card defines genetic load as "the existence of deleterious genes within a population" — the board wording. The population-genetics definition is the reduction in a population's mean fitness caused by those alleles. Confirm the board wording is what is wanted before anyone "improves" it.
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_biogenetic_law`: the card gives the law and both its names but never names **Ernst Haeckel**. Does the key expect the proposer for the 1M "the law" split?
- `ts_ipe_z2_oev_connecting_links`: the question asks only for names. Does the key in fact require the two groups beside each name (the card's insider note asserts it does — filed WRONG above)?
