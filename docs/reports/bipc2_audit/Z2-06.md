## Teacher-gate questions
- **Yellow spot vs fovea.** NCERT assigns the densely packed, rod-free cone region to the **fovea** (the central pit of the macula lutea), not to the whole yellow spot; Indian Intermediate texts routinely teach "the yellow spot contains only cones". The card follows the Indian convention in its boxed line ("Only cones, so it gives sharp central vision") and in `s2_yellow MISTAKE[0]` ("Giving the yellow spot rods. It contains only cones."). Recorded as a convention, not filed as an error — a board teacher should rule whether the fovea must be named, and whether a script that mentions rods in the macula outside the fovea can be called a mistake.
