## Minor notes (not verdict-changing)

1. **fig_14** — step s1 says the anode potential "can be made positive or negative with respect to C", but the drawn loop is a single cell in series with a rheostat, so only the magnitude of the retarding potential can be varied; reversing the sign of the anode potential needs a potential-divider tapping with a reversing/commutator key. The drawn polarity is the right one for the question actually asked (measuring V₀), so the figure is not wrong for the answer it serves — but it cannot do what s1's sentence claims. A text-side call for the card owner.
2. **fig_18** — the E₂ arrowhead is detached from its shaft (shaft ends at x≈246, head tip at x≈228; the ~11 px gap renders as a visible break), whereas E₁'s head is attached. Also, no dot marks the point P and the centre O is not lettered, even though the step's mistake note tells the student that r is measured from O.
3. **fig_27** — the force on −q is drawn but left unlabelled; only the +q force carries "qE", although the margin note speaks of "the two opposite forces".
4. **fig_21** — the diagram step's "why" says the two arrows lean "on either side of the **vertical**". As drawn (correctly, and as step s4 requires) they are mirror images about the **horizontal**: the components perpendicular to the dipole axis cancel and the axial components add. The figure is right; that sentence is not.
