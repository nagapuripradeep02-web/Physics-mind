## Teacher-gate questions

1. **Popoff's rule — which direction does the Telangana board expect?** `ts_ipe_c2_akc_oxidation_products_of_carbonyl_compounds` s1_principle now prints "For an unsymmetrical ketone the bond on the smaller side breaks, so the carbonyl carbon stays with the **larger** group (Popoff's rule)", and s4_acetophenone's `why` applies it consistently. The commoner statement in Indian texts is the opposite — "the keto group remains with the **smaller** alkyl group". Per the brief this is recorded here, not as a finding. Two things the teacher should note when ruling: (a) the **products are certain either way** — acetophenone gives benzoic acid, and every equation on the card is balanced and correct; (b) the two wordings diverge on a purely aliphatic case such as pentan-2-one (card's version → butanoic acid; "smaller group" version → acetic + propanoic acid, which is what is observed), so whichever wording the board expects should be applied fleet-wide. It is also worth deciding whether acetophenone belongs under Popoff at all, since an aryl methyl ketone's oxidation to benzoic acid is side-chain oxidation of the ring compound rather than the aliphatic C–C cleavage the rule describes. (The card's own verification note already raises this; the 2026-08-30 Vidi correction did remove a genuine self-contradiction and the card is now internally consistent.)
2. **"Tollen's" vs "Tollens'".** `ts_ipe_c2_akc_tollens_reagent_with_aldehyde` deliberately keeps the book's "Tollen's" in `question_text` and writes "Tollens'" everywhere in the answer. Confirm which spelling the board prints and whether the mismatch inside one card is acceptable.
3. **Pyridine in acetylation.** `ts_ipe_c2_akc_cannizzaro_and_acetylation` s4 `why` and `ts_ipe_c2_akc_acetylation_cannizzaro_cross_aldol_decarboxylation` s1 `why` say the ethanol and aniline cases "need no catalyst". NCERT carries both out "in the presence of a base such as pyridine" (an HCl scavenger, not a catalyst — so the card is defensible). Confirm whether the board expects pyridine named on the answer sheet.
4. **Every mark split in this group is the bank's claim.** All 14 cards carry `verification.status = "unverified"` and `needs_teacher_verification: true`, and each note records that no second-year chemistry board paper and no second source book were available, so neither a union check nor a back-test was possible. The 1+1 (VSAQ), 1+1+1+1 (SAQ) and 2+2+2+2 (LAQ) splits need a board teacher's confirmation.
