## Teacher-gate questions

1. **Nitrile hydrolysis under H⁺.** `ts_ipe_c2_ocn_diazonium_to_benzamide_abc` s1_a_and_b prints
   C₆H₅CN + 2H₂O → C₆H₅COOH + NH₃ with the condition [H⁺, hydrolysis]. The equation balances and is
   the form most Indian texts print, but in acid the ammonia is present as NH₄⁺ (some books write
   "…→ C₆H₅COOH + NH₄⁺"). Should the bank print NH₃ (book convention, kept here) or NH₄⁺?
2. **Aniline solubility figure.** The card prints "about 3.4 g in 100 g of water at 293 K". Sources
   quote 3.4–3.6 g/100 g between 293 K and 298 K, and NCERT prints no number at all. Is a
   non-NCERT figure wanted inside a boxed final step, and if so which value does the board accept?
3. **Isocyanide name for (iv).** `ts_ipe_c2_ocn_iupac_names_four_compounds` s4 offers three forms —
   "4-bromobenzyl isocyanide", "4-bromophenylmethyl carbylamine", "1-bromo-4-(isocyanomethyl)benzene".
   All three are defensible and `margin_note` says any one earns the mark. A Telangana examiner
   should confirm the board accepts the fully systematic third form, which is not the printed one.
4. **The whole unit's 1+1 mark split** remains the bank's own claim: the source book prints no mark
   scheme, and (as every `verification_note` in this group records) **neither the two-book union
   check nor a board back-test was possible for Chemistry-II** — the TSBIE Basic Learning Material
   on hand is physics-only and first-year, and no second-year chemistry paper is in the corpus.
   Every mark split in this group is unverified against a real paper.
