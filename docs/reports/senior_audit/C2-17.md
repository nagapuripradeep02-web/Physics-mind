Cards audited: 12 / 12   Findings: HARMFUL 0 · WRONG 3 · WEAK 6

Every card was solved from `QUESTION:` before its steps were read. A python script
(`balance.py`) atom- and charge-balanced all seven printed equations (three XeF₆ hydrolyses,
the Bartlett reaction, the three fluoride preparations) — all seven balance, charge 0 = 0 —
and re-derived the σ-bond + lone-pair count, hybridisation, electron-pair arrangement and
VSEPR shape for XeO₃, XeF₂, XeF₄, XeF₆ and XeOF₄ from Xe's eight valence electrons: all five
reproduce the card exactly (sp³/pyramidal, sp³d/linear, sp³d²/square planar, sp³d³/distorted
octahedral, sp³d²/square pyramidal). A second script (`struct.py`) read the raw JSON and
checked step-mark sums, `mark_split` sums, label pairing and per-row marks (12/12 clean),
scanned for duplicated prose across the group, and listed every reader-facing "the book"
reference. Standard values were checked against NCERT Class 12 Ch.7: IE(Xe) 1170 and IE(O₂)
1175 kJ mol⁻¹, Δ_egH large and positive, F 4.0 / O 3.5 / N and Cl 3.0, Ar 0.93% of air,
He bp ≈ 4 K, N₂ bp 77 K, and all nine preparation conditions (673 K/1 bar/Xe excess;
873 K/7 bar/1:5; 573 K/60–70 bar/1:20). Figure strokes were read coordinate by coordinate.

## Teacher-gate questions

1. **573 K vs 600 K, and "Xe in excess" vs 1 : 3, for the xenon fluorides.** The card follows
   NCERT against the state's own FAST TRACK book on both. A Telangana IPE teacher must confirm
   which figures the board's key marks — this is the one place in the group where a student
   following the card writes a *different number* from the printed state guide.
2. **Helium in aeroplane tyres.** The source book prints it; the card refuses it as unsourced
   (correctly, on the physical facts). Does the board's key accept it as a listed use?
3. **The name "xenon hexafluoroplatinate(V)".** The book prints "xenon hexafluoro Platinate"
   with no oxidation number. Does the board require, accept, or penalise the (V)?
4. **XeO₃ bond angle 103°.** Correct experimentally and printed in the source book, but not an
   NCERT figure. Is it expected for the second mark, or is "pyramidal" alone enough?
5. **The 0-mark diagram steps.** Both SAQ cards assert that the drawings earn nothing because
   the question says "explain, not draw". If the board's key does give a diagram mark, four
   marks are resting on the wrong steps in two cards.
6. **All mark splits in this unit are the bank's claim, not the board's** — every
   `verification_note` says so, and no second-year chemistry paper or second book was available
   for a union check or a back-test. The whole unit is unverified on marking.
