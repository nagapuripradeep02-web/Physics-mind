## Teacher-gate questions

1. **`ts_ipe_c2_va_iron_passive_conc_hno3` — which oxide, and which equation, does the board's key
   want?** The source book prints `Fe + 2HNO₃ → FeO + H₂O + 2NO₂` with FeO as the passive film. The
   card deliberately does not follow it and writes `3Fe + 8HNO₃ → Fe₃O₄ + 8NO₂ + 4H₂O` instead. Both
   equations balance (verified), and the card is chemically the sounder of the two — conc. HNO₃ is
   far too strong an oxidant to leave iron at +2, and Fe₃O₄ is what the corrosion literature and most
   Indian texts name. But **NCERT Class 12 names no oxide at all** (it says only that conc. HNO₃
   renders iron passive by forming a passive film of oxide), and the card's substitute equation is
   printed in no textbook I can point a student at. A Telangana IPE teacher must rule on whether an
   examiner marking from the book's key will accept Fe₃O₄, and whether the card should print the
   book's FeO line as an accepted alternative rather than only recording it in a `why`.
2. **Haber's process — book description vs NCERT description.** The card writes finely divided iron
   with a molybdenum promoter at 450–500 °C / 200–300 atm (the book), and records NCERT's iron oxide
   with K₂O and Al₂O₃ promoters at ~200 atm / ~700 K in the `why`, saying "either description is
   accepted". Confirm that both are in fact accepted in the IPE key.
3. **Ostwald Step I conditions and Step III form.** The card prints the book's ~800 °C / 6–7 atm and
   the air-fed `4NO₂ + 2H₂O + O₂ → 4HNO₃`; NCERT gives ~500 K / 9 bar and `3NO₂ + H₂O → 2HNO₃ + NO`
   with the NO recycled. Both Step III forms balance and both are marked on the card as acceptable —
   confirm the board agrees.
4. **"Fuming nitric acid" for 98 % HNO₃** (`ostwalds_process`, s4_concentration). This is the usual
   Indian-textbook usage, but strictly *fuming* nitric acid is the acid carrying dissolved NO₂ (red
   fuming) or ≥97.5 % white fuming acid. Not filed as a finding; confirm the board's usage.
5. **Mark splits throughout the group are the bank's own claim**, as every `VERIFICATION_NOTE` says.
   The 1+1 (VSAQ), 1×4 (SAQ) and 1+2+2+1+1+1 / 1×8 (LAQ) splits are internally consistent but
   unconfirmed by the board.
