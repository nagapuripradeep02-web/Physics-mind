## Teacher-gate questions

- **`ts_ipe_m2a_pb_truth_75_80_statements_do_not_match`** — step `s1_events` asserts "A,
  B... are independent" and every later step (s2, s3) relies on that independence to
  multiply probabilities, but the `QUESTION` text ("A speaks truth in 75% of the cases
  and B in 80% of the cases…") never uses the word "independent"/"independently" — unlike
  every other independence card in this group (`independent_0_6_0_7_four_parts`,
  `independent_0_2_0_5_four_parts`, `problem_solved_by_at_least_one_1_3_1_4`,
  `three_independent_events_find_p_a_b_c`, `complements_of_independent_events_independent`),
  all of which have "independent"/"independently" printed in their own `QUESTION`. I did
  not grade this WRONG/HARMFUL because: (a) it is a universally standard textbook
  archetype — the problem is unsolvable from the two given marginal percentages without
  assuming independence, and no other variant of this problem exists in Indian board
  sources; (b) the book itself (Baby Bullet-Q, confirmed in `VERIFICATION_NOTE`) solves
  it the same way and the card's numeric answer (7/20) matches; (c) unlike
  `contractor_both_contracts_2_3_5_9_4_5` — where the group correctly WARNS against
  assuming independence because the stem instead grants `P(A∪B)` — there is no
  alternative data here that independence would override. A teacher should confirm
  whether the `WHY`/`MISTAKE` fields ought to name this as "the standard convention for
  this problem type" rather than stating it as a bare given, for a sharp student who
  asks why.
