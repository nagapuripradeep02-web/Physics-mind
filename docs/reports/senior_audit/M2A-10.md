| ts_ipe_m2a_pf_2x2_plus_2x_plus_1_over_x3_plus_x2 | s1_form | `common_mistakes[1]`: "Treating x² as an irreducible quadratic and writing (Ax + B)/x²." ## Teacher-gate questions

Whether boxing "A = …, B = …, C = …" (as these three long-answer cards do, because the full
resolved expression runs to 3–4 lines) is acceptable exam presentation for the "write the result"
mark, or whether the assembled expression itself must be the boxed line — a Telangana IPE teacher
should rule on this presentation convention. (This sits inside the wider caveat every card in the
unit already carries: the 1+1+1+1 mark split is the bank's own claim, unverified against a board
answer key.) No genuinely divergent board convention (the thiocyanato/Popoff's-rule kind of case)
arises in a partial-fractions unit — nothing else to gate.
