## Teacher-gate questions

1. **Vitamin E deficiency (the HARMFUL row) — the one item a board teacher must rule on.** The source
   guide ("FAST TRACK IPE for Sr. Students", book p.103) prints "Sterility, Neurosis of heart muscles";
   NCERT Table 14.5 prints "Increased fragility of RBCs and muscular weakness". Which does the Telangana
   IPE key accept? If the key is the NCERT table, this card currently costs the student that mark. If the
   guide's wording is genuinely the accepted answer, the card should still print both.
2. **Vitamin E sources.** The card gives "vegetable oils such as sunflower oil, green leafy vegetables,
   egg yolk". NCERT gives "vegetable oils like wheat germ oil, sunflower oil". Should wheat germ oil be
   named (it is the NCERT head of the list), and are the two extras accepted?
3. **"Cellulose forms the cell wall of plants and of bacteria"** (`importance_of_carbohydrates` s2_structural,
   point 3). This is NCERT Chemistry Ch. 14 verbatim ("Cell wall of bacteria and plants is made up of
   cellulose") and is therefore *not* filed as a finding — but a bacterial cell wall is peptidoglycan, which
   is what the same student's Botany paper teaches. The bank now contains both statements. A teacher should
   decide whether the chemistry card keeps NCERT's wording as-is (defensible: it is the prescribed text) or
   adds a half-line reconciling it.
4. **Enzyme numbers** (`enzymes` s3_rate). "About ten million times" for the rate enhancement and "pH of
   about 6 to 8" for the working range are the guide's figures; other Indian texts print 10⁶ substrate
   molecules per minute and an optimum pH of 5 to 7. The card's `VERIFICATION_NOTE` already flags the rate
   figure as an order of magnitude. Does the board key fix either number?
5. **The cyclic-glucose drawing** (`structure_of_glucose` s5_figure_cyclic). The card draws the pyranose
   form as a Fischer projection with an oxygen bracket from C1 to C5, not as a Haworth ring — an explicit,
   documented choice (the notebook renderer cannot draw a Haworth perspective honestly). It is chemically
   correct and it is what the source book prints. Would a Telangana examiner expect the Haworth ring?
