## Teacher-gate questions

- **Single arrow vs ⇌ for the SHE electrode reaction.** NCERT prints H⁺(aq) + e⁻ → ½H₂(g) with a
  single arrow; this bank teaches 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ ⇌ H₂ and marks the single arrow as an error. Does the
  Telangana model answer require the reversible arrow? If it does not, the WRONG row above stands
  as written; if it does, the item survives but should be softened to a preference.
- **"Daniel" vs "Daniell".** NCERT spells it Daniell. Confirm before the bank-wide sweep, in case
  the Telangana textbook prints the single-l form and students are marked against that.
- **298 K in the SHE definition.** NCERT assigns the SHE zero potential *at all temperatures*, yet
  every Indian text (and this card) lists 298 K as a standard condition. The card's
  `common_mistakes[1]` says an electrode without 298 K "is not a STANDARD one". Left un-filed as an
  accepted textbook simplification — a teacher should confirm the board wants 298 K listed.
- **"Equivalent mass" vs "equivalent weight".** The source book prints "equivalent weights"; the
  cards use "equivalent mass" and keep the old word only in the mistakes list. Confirm the board
  accepts either.
- **0.0591 vs 0.059.** The source book prints both (VSAQ 10 vs LAQ 2); this unit standardises on
  0.0591, which is the correctly rounded value (computed 0.059128 V). Confirm nothing in the board
  scheme expects 0.059.
- **The mark splits themselves.** Every `VERIFICATION_NOTE` in this unit says the 1+1 (and 1+1+1+1)
  split is the bank's claim and not the board's. That is still open; this audit checked only their
  internal consistency, which is clean.

## Notes

- **Chemically this group is in good shape.** Nine equations, all atom- and charge-balanced by
  script; every constant, unit and worked number reproduced. Not one printed LINE or boxed answer
  is wrong, which is why the HARMFUL column is empty. Both WRONG findings are in prose (`why`,
  `common_mistakes`), the pattern the maths audits also found.
- **The source-book corrections claimed in the `VERIFICATION_NOTE`s were re-derived and are all
  themselves right:** ΔG = −nFE_cell paired with ΔG° = −nFE°_cell (the book mixed the degree signs);
  S m⁻¹ with a capital S, and mho named as the older non-SI word; conductivity defined on a unit
  *cube* rather than "1 m³ or 1 cm³"; Λm given the symbol Λm and defined as a *conductance*, with M
  named as the molarity; Λ°m = ν₊λ°₊ + ν₋λ°₋ with the BaCl₂ example the book's 1:1-only form gets
  wrong; "quantity of electricity" in Faraday's first law against the book's self-contradicting
  "quantity of current"; and the excision of the secondary-battery text spliced into the book's
  fuel-cell answer. The fuel-cell efficiency figures (≈70% vs ≈40% for a thermal plant) match NCERT.
- **Cross-card duplication is deliberate, not a defect.** Four of these VSAQs are paired with an LAQ
  in the same unit (SHE, Nernst, Kohlrausch, batteries), and the paired cards share memory tips and
  mistakes items nearly verbatim. The `VERIFICATION_NOTE`s declare the pairing and the division of
  labour, so this was not filed — but note that a defect in a shared string is therefore a
  **two-card** defect, which is how both WRONG rows above landed.
- **Checked and accepted, not filed:** (a) `standard_hydrogen_electrode` s2 prints "the emf of a
  cell made with it is the standard electrode potential of the other electrode" without the
  "provided that electrode is also at standard conditions" caveat — the whole card is framed at
  standard conditions and NCERT's own wording is barely tighter; (b) `molar_conductivity` s1 uses
  "current carrying power", which is the phrasing Indian texts use for conducting power and not a
  Rule-41 metaphor; (c) `conductivity_definition` s1's "the shape drops out" reads as ordinary
  mathematical English; (d) `primary_and_secondary_batteries` s1 `common_mistakes[1]` ("saying it
  has no electrolyte") is an unusual but real confusion, given "dry" cell.
- **Structure is clean across all 11 cards:** every `mark_split` label matches its step label in
  order, every split entry matches its step's marks, all running totals are contiguous
  (0→1→2, and 0→1→2→3→4 on the SAQ), and every total equals `marks_total`. `expected_time_min` is
  2 min per mark throughout the unit (VSAQ 2/4, SAQ 4/8, LAQ 8/15) — this group is in line.
- **For whoever audits part 2/2 (the LAQs):** `ts_ipe_c2_ec_types_of_batteries` carries the
  ammonia-escapes error as a printed LINE ("It cannot be recharged, because the ammonia escapes and
