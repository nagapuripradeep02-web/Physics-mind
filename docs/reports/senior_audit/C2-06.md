## HARMFUL — wrong chemistry a student would copy into an exam

_(none)_

Every boxed answer in the group is correct. The re-derivations that matter: Λ°m(HAc) = 425.9 + 91.0
− 126.4 = 390.5 S cm² mol⁻¹; m(Cu) = 31.75 × 900 / 96500 = 0.296 g; m(Cu) = 31.75 × 1200 / 96500 =
0.395 g (the source book's 31 → 0.39 g is genuinely wrong — recomputed 0.3855 g from the book's own
number, so the book is not even self-consistent, and the card's correction to 0.395 g is right);
E(Zn, 0.1 M Zn²⁺) = −0.76 + (0.0591/2)(−1) = −0.79 V; E°cell(Daniell) = 0.34 − (−0.76) = 1.10 V;
E°cell(rusting) = 1.23 − (−0.44) = 1.67 V; F = 6.022×10²³ × 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ = 9.647×10⁴ C mol⁻¹.
The four **book errors the cards claim to correct were each re-derived and the corrections are
themselves right**: the "external battery" on the Daniell cell (a galvanic cell is joined by a wire
through a voltmeter — an external battery would make it an electrolytic cell), "decomposes into its
IONS" (the ions are already present; electrolysis discharges them), "proportional to the quantity of
CURRENT" (it is the quantity of electricity Q = It — the book's own next line contains the time), and
the missing stoichiometric numbers in Kohlrausch's law.

## Teacher-gate questions

1. **Every mark split in this unit is the bank's own claim.** All ten `VERIFICATION_NOTE`s say so
   explicitly (the source book prints no scheme). A Telangana IPE teacher must confirm 1+1+1+1 (SAQ),
   1+2+2+2+1 and 2+2+1+1+1+1 (LAQ), and 1+1 (VSAQ) — and in particular whether the Daniell-cell and
   SHE sketches really carry 2 marks each while the dry-cell sketch in
   `ts_ipe_c2_ec_types_of_batteries` carries **0** (the card's reasoning — "explain the construction",
   not "draw" — is sound, but it is an inference from the question wording, not a scheme).
2. **1 bar vs 1 atm for the SHE.** The card writes 1 bar (current NCERT standard state); the source
   book's text says 1 atm and its own VSAQ says "1 atm or 1 bar". If the board's scheme is written to
   1 atm, confirm 1 bar is accepted.
3. **"Normal hydrogen electrode" as a synonym for SHE** (`she_construction_working` s1). Strictly NHE
   (unit *concentration*) and SHE (unit *activity*, 1 bar) are not the same electrode, though Indian
   texts almost always equate them. Confirm the board treats them as one.
4. **Dry-cell cathode mechanism** (see the WRONG row): NCERT's single step MnO₂ + NH₄⁺ + e⁻ →
   MnO(OH) + NH₃, or the classical two-step depolariser account NH₄⁺ + e⁻ → NH₃ + ½H₂ then
   H₂ + 2MnO₂ → Mn₂O₃ + H₂O? Both appear in Indian texts. Which does the board's answer expect?
5. **Salt-bridge electrolyte** (`galvanic_cell_daniel_cell` s2): the card specifies KCl in agar. KNO₃
   and NH₄NO₃ are equally standard. Confirm KCl is accepted for a Daniell cell.
6. **0.0591 vs 0.059** — the source book prints both on facing pages; the card standardises on 0.0591
   throughout (recomputed 2.303RT/F = 0.059128 V at 298 K, so 0.0591 is the better value). Confirm
   the board does not mark to 0.059.
7. **"Daniel cell" vs "Daniell cell."** The question text (sourced, not invented) and every card line
   use the single-l spelling. Confirm this is what the board prints and accepts.
8. **`ts_ipe_c2_ec_prob_copper_deposited_2a` placement.** Its source is a boxed "PROBLEM" belonging to
   no printed paper section; both the Section-A/VSAQ placement and the 2-mark weight are authored
   claims (the card's own note says so). Confirm a 2-mark VSAQ of this shape actually appears.

## Notes

- **Chemically this group is in strong shape.** 24/24 equations balance in atoms *and* charge;
  every numeric, every standard potential (Fe²⁺/Fe −0.44, O₂/H₂O +1.23, Zn²⁺/Zn −0.76, Cu²⁺/Cu
  +0.34, H⁺/H₂ 0.00), every oxidation state and every unit reproduces. Zero wrong products, zero
  wrong reagents, zero reversed electrode signs, zero wrong final answers.
- **Electrode-sign conventions are right everywhere and are the group's strongest feature.** The
  galvanic cell has anode negative / cathode positive; the electrolytic cell has cathode negative
  (joined to the battery's negative terminal); `electrolysis_and_faraday_first_law` s2 states the
  contrast explicitly and correctly ("reduction is always at the cathode; only the sign changes").
  E°cell = cathode − anode is used consistently and its inverse is correctly flagged as a mistake.
- **The book-correction claims all hold up.** Each of the four substantive corrections was
  re-derived from scratch (see HARMFUL section). The added rust equation
  2Fe²⁺ + 2H₂O + ½O₂ → Fe₂O₃ + 4H⁺, which the source book omits entirely, balances at 2 Fe / 3 O /
  4 H and +4 charge on both sides and matches the NCERT form.
