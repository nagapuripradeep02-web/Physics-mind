## Notes

- **The two source-book corrections named in the brief are both confirmed correct, machine-verified
  from raw geometry, not just re-read prose.** (1) pnp/npn arrow swap
  (`transistor_circuit_symbols`, and reused in `transistor_and_its_working`'s connection figures):
  a dot-product test on the SVG stroke coordinates confirms the npn emitter-arrow tip points
  outward and the pnp tip points inward, and that the pnp battery's emitter-side plate is the
  longer (positive) one while the npn battery's emitter-side plate is the shorter (negative) one,
  with the current arrows pointing into the pnp emitter and out of the npn emitter — all matching
  the physics and the card's stated correction of the book's page-54 swap. (2) NOR gate mis-captioned
  as NAND (`nand_and_nor_gates`): the card's NOR equation `Y = complement(A+B)` and truth table
  (1,0,0,0) are both independently correct and distinct from the NAND equation/table it replaces.
- **40.6% / 81.2% are the verbatim NCERT/board textbook maxima for half-wave/full-wave rectifier
  efficiency**, reproduced identically across five cards with no typos. A symbolic re-derivation
  (sympy, from first principles: Idc = Im/π, Irms = Im/2) gives the exact constants 4/π² = 40.53%
  and 8/π² = 81.06%, i.e. the textbook figures are themselves rounded generously (40.53→"40.6",
  81.06→"81.2") rather than to the nearest 0.1%. This is the universal Indian-board convention, not
  a card-specific error, so it is not filed as a finding — flagged here for the record.
- **β = α/(1−α)** in `transistor_and_its_working` was re-derived symbolically from
  I_E = I_B + I_C, α = I_C/I_E, β = I_C/I_B and confirmed to match exactly (zero symbolic
  difference).
- **All four gate truth tables** (AND, OR, NAND, NOR) and the NOT-gate table were brute-force
  regenerated and match every card's printed rows exactly; the "universal gate" claim that AND+OR
  alone cannot build a NOT gate was confirmed with a monotone-Boolean-function check (AND and OR
  are monotone, NOT is not, so no composition of AND/OR alone can realize it).
- **Every `MARK_SPLIT` sum was checked against `marks_total`** across all 22 cards (2-, 4- and
  8-mark cards) and all match; every 0-mark diagram step correctly carries no `MARK_SPLIT` row.
  `expected_time_min` is consistent within qtype across the whole group (VSAQ→4, SAQ→8, LAQ→15).

  `MARGIN_NOTE` in this group was left alone — it is a house convention used group-wide (and, per
  the maths-audit sample, elsewhere in the bank too), not a Rule 41 personification of the mark
  itself doing something physical.
