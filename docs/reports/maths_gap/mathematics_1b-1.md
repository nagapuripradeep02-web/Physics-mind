## Notes
The four EXTRA cards are the ellipse/hyperbola-defining loci (sum and difference of
distances). The book's Locus chapter does not carry that family at all, so they came
from the older Baby Bullet-Q source. They are inventory, not a defect — but note the
asymmetry: we are strong exactly where the book is silent, and thin on the book's
symbolic case (saq11) and its two easiest one-star drills.
