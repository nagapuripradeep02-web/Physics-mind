**Sub-topic breakdown (all 5 sub-topics fully covered, none thin):**
- **Errors and Approximations** (17/17 matched) — both drill families present: linearisation
  (dy vs Δy for 5 functions) and root/trig approximation (∛999, √82, cos(60°5′), √65, ∛7.8), plus
  percentage-error word problems (square, pendulum, sphere ×2) and the general theorem (y = kxⁿ).
- **Rate Measure** (19/19 matched) — related-rates geometry (cone×2, cube×2, circle, ripple,
  balloon, bubble), a moving-point-on-a-curve case, marginal cost/revenue, bacteria growth (both the
  t^(3/2) and t³ forms), and six rectilinear-motion (velocity/acceleration) problems.
- **Tangents and Normals** (27/27 matched) — the largest sub-topic and fully covered across every
  method: implicit/parametric tangent-normal equations, subtangent/subnormal/tangent-length/
  normal-length formulas (algebraic and parametric curves), angle-between-two-curves (5 pairs,
  including the fourth-quadrant-restricted case), orthogonality, and curves-touching-at-a-point.
- **Maxima and Minima** (10/10 matched) — both inscribed-solid optimisation problems (cylinder in
  sphere, cylinder in cone), the classic box/rectangle optimisation pair, two-number constrained
  optimisation (sum fixed, product/power maximised), absolute extrema on a closed interval,
  increasing/decreasing intervals, and a profit-maximisation word problem.
- **Rolle's and Lagrange's Theorem** (16/16 matched) — the theorem statements, six Rolle's
  verifications of increasing difficulty (polynomial, trig, exponential-weighted, logarithmic), the
  two "prove more than one c" / "find a and b from a given c" reasoning questions, four LMVT
  verifications, and the two geometric corollaries (tangent parallel to a chord; no k gives two
  roots).
