tan⁻¹ solve-for-x equation) — it is the chapter's most complex printed item and has no authored
counterpart or near-variant anywhere in our 10 cards. All matches were made on the underlying
sin⁻¹/cos⁻¹/tan⁻¹ identity (the specific fractions and RHS target), never on wording.
