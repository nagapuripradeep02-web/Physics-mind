## Notes
The shape of this unit's gap is the clearest in either paper: **all three of our EXTRA
cards are second copies of a method we already cover** (a second intercepts drill, a
second plane-from-intercepts, a second parallel-plane), while five genuinely different
plane constructions have no card at all. Authoring here is not about volume — it is about
replacing duplication with the missing archetypes, starting with the 2-star point-and-normal
form (vsaq8) and the definition (vsaq12), which is free marks a student loses for nothing.
