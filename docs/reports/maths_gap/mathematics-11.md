## Notes
The book prints Chapter 11 as a single VSAQ block (no SAQ, no LAQ) with 14 distinct items across 11
numbered entries, two of which (vsaq9, vsaq10) are printed as "Theorem" rather than numbered exercises
but are asked and worked identically. Our unit's 10 cards match 7 of those 14 exactly; the other 3 slots
went to identities the book does not list as separate exercises in this chapter (a duplicate-technique
tanh⁻¹ instance, and two standard identities/double-angle formulas that read as background-theory
statements rather than book-indexed questions). The MISSING list is weighted toward the two ADDITION
FORMULAS (vsaq8i/8ii, sinh/cosh of x+y) and the two GENERAL inverse-log THEOREMS (vsaq9, vsaq10,
sinh⁻¹x and tanh⁻¹x in closed form) — all four are foundational, frequently-examined identities with no
authored card at all, making them the highest-value authoring targets. All matches were made on the
underlying identity/derivation, never on wording.
