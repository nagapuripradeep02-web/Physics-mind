Substituting `cos²X = 1 − sin²X` into the book's identity reduces it to EXACTLY the card's statement in one line, so solving one solves the other — I called this MATCHED rather than MISSING, but flag it because a founder skimming stems side by side would not see the match without doing that substitution themselves.
