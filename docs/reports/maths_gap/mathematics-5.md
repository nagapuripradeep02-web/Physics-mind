## Notes

**Method-vs-data discipline.** Matrices is the largest unit on both sides (55 cards / 83 book
questions), and most of the risk is in the LAQ block, where the book asks the SAME system by up to
three different methods (matrix inversion / Cramer's rule / Gauss-Jordan) as one combined question,
while our bank authors each method as its own separate card. Two systems (2x−y+3z=9,x+y+z=6,x−y+z=2
and 3x+4y+5z=18,2x−y+8z=13,5x−2y+7z=20) are fully covered this way and filed under ELSEWHERE
(laq9i, laq9iv) rather than MATCHED, since no single card carries all three methods. Two more
systems are only partially covered — laq9iii has Cramer but not Gauss-Jordan, laq9vii has inversion
but not Cramer/Gauss-Jordan (filed PARTIAL). laq9ii has no card by any method (MISSING).

**The laq9v / laq10 near-miss (the most important finding in this report).** The book's laq9v
stem carries a PRINTED DEFECT (the source index's own note): the printed third equation reads
−x + 3y + 3z = 5, but the book's own working matrix and answer are only consistent with
−x + 3y + z = 5 (determinant 50, solution (0,1,2)) — that corrected version is what "laq9v" means
here. Our card ts_ipe_m1a_mat_cramer_x_minus_y_3z_5 (laq10) reads almost identically
(x−y+3z=5, 4x+2y−z=0, x+3y+z=5) but its THIRD equation has +x, not −x. Verified by determinant:
the book's corrected system gives det=50; our card's system gives det=40. These are two different
systems with two different solutions — solving one does not solve the other. Do not treat laq10 as
covering laq9v when authoring; laq9v is a genuine MISSING card, and laq10 stays in EXTRA (it may
still be worth keeping — the Baby Bullet-Q system is a legitimate independent drill — but it is not
board-sourced coverage for laq9v).

**Pure-definition VSAQs are a systematic gap.** The book carries seven stand-alone "define X"
one-mark questions (rank vsaq19, adjoint vsaq22, inverse vsaq23, triangular matrix vsaq28,
singular/non-singular vsaq29 — plus symmetric/skew-symmetric vsaq14, whose content is at least
embedded in vsaq11/vsaq12 today) with no matching card. These are cheap, high-value 0–1-mark VSAQ
authoring — bookwork with no algebra to check.

**The rank block (vsaq20 i–v, vsaq21) is the weakest sub-topic.** The book asks six rank
computations: an all-ones matrix (MATCHED vsaq17), one more 3×3 that is genuinely rank-2 (MATCHED
vsaq18/book-vsaq21), and FOUR more (two full-rank 3×3, one 3×4, one 2×3) with no card at all. We
have zero coverage of rank on a non-square (rectangular) matrix — a distinct skill (row-reduce to
echelon form on an m×n shape) worth its own authored card rather than a "different data" note.

**The one word problem in the chapter (vsaq30) has no analog anywhere in the unit.** Every other
card in both the book and our bank is pure algebraic manipulation; this is the only applied/word
framing, and per the source index's own note it will need a Rule-35 neutral restatement of the
currency amounts when authored.

**Section-length mismatches (ELSEWHERE, 3 of 6) are all real coverage, just filed at a different
depth than the book.** vsaq14, saq9i and saq18i are genuinely answered — the second two by exact
numeric match, just at VSAQ length where the book asks them at SAQ length. These are low-priority
for the authoring queue (already covered) but worth knowing about if a length-fidelity pass is ever
scoped.
