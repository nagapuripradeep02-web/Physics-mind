## Judgement calls

1. **vsaq10 (diver) and vsaq15 (platform+person) vs. bank vsaq16 (ballet dancer):** all three test the identical L=Iω-conservation-with-changing-I relationship, but each is a differently-named real-world scenario. Treated as MISSING rather than MATCHED, on the view that an authored card is normally scenario-specific — but a case could be made that the dancer card already teaches the transferable physics.
2. **vsaq9 book (spanner) analog vs. book's own grinder/steering-wheel/jack-screw questions (vsaq12/13/14):** all four are "longer moment arm needs less force for the same torque," each a different named object. Treated as four distinct gaps rather than one physics point with three restatements — bank's existing spanner card (vsaq9) was not used to cover any of them.
3. **saq12b (book, "write the expression for angular momentum") vs. two candidate bank rows:** matched to bank vsaq15 (VSAQ-depth definition + L=Iω relation) rather than bank saq13 (full first-principles derivation of L=Iω), on the "essentially the same depth" criterion — this left saq13 as EXTRA. A stricter reader might instead call saq13 the closer intellectual match and leave vsaq15 unused.
4. **saq12a (torque expression) vs. bank saq6 (τ=Iα relation):** not matched, since saq6's derived expression is the rotational-dynamics analogue of F=ma, not the general τ = r×F / rFsinθ form a bare "write the expression for torque" question is presumed to want.
5. **saq12d (rotational KE expression) vs. bank saq14 (KE of rolling body):** not matched — rolling KE combines translational and rotational terms and is a materially different derivation from the bare ½Iω² the book asks for.
6. **prob5 (book, MI of 3 equal particles on a triangle) vs. bank saq8 (CM of 3 unequal particles on a triangle):** not matched — different quantity computed (MI vs. CM) and different given data, despite the shared "particles at triangle vertices" setup.
7. **vsaq9/vsaq12 partial match:** bank vsaq12 was judged to answer only the "define + relate to I" half of the book's two-part ask; the "factors it depends on" half remains an authoring gap even though the row is counted PARTIAL rather than MISSING.
