## Judgement calls

- **laq4 vs bank saq2**: classified SECTION-MISMATCH rather than MISSING. Book laq4 explicitly demands two deliverables — proving the spring-block motion is SHM, then deriving the time-period equation. Bank saq2's stated deliverable is only the frequency equation; it likely passes through the SHM force law (F = −ky) as a derivation step, but does not explicitly claim to prove SHM or state a time-period formula as its object. Treat this as unresolved pending a look at the actual saq2 card body.
- **prob3 vs bank vsaq6**: classified SECTION-MISMATCH. Book asks "which physical quantities have their maximum value at the mean position" (answer: velocity, kinetic energy); bank vsaq6 asks "where is v max / where is a max, and their max values" — physically equivalent information but framed from the opposite direction, and vsaq6 doesn't explicitly name kinetic energy. Kept as a match; flag if a stricter read is wanted.
- **vsaq7 (book, pendulum at centre of earth)**: kept MISSING. physics-8 (Gravitation) vsaq4 (`ts_ipe_p1_grav_g_at_depth`) supplies the needed fact (g = 0 at the centre) but does not itself answer the pendulum question — noted as a cross-reference, not counted as ELSEWHERE.
- **prob5 vs bank saq8** and **prob14 vs bank saq7**: both kept as non-matches (MISSING / PARTIAL respectively) on the "same given data" rule — same formula/topic, different numbers or missing the numeric step, so a student is not actually served by the existing card.
- **vsaq4/vsaq8/saq4 (bank, EXTRA rows)**: "topic present: yes" verdicts are judgement calls — the underlying physics is used throughout the chapter's other answers even though the book never asks it as its own standalone question.
