Every book entry still gets exactly one status; PARTIAL rows are excluded from MATCHED, SECTION-MISMATCH, ELSEWHERE, and MISSING counts.
- **Matching required the same specific molecule/ion**, per the method ("same molecule, same theory"). This produced several close-but-not-matching calls treated as MISSING rather than a match: VSEPR shape of ClF₃ vs bank's CO₂/SO₂ card (vsaq12); zero-dipole H₂O/CO₂ vs bank's BF₃ card (saq20); resonance of CO₃²⁻ vs bank's ozone card (prob3, saq11 stays EXTRA). A looser "same theory" reading would flip these to PARTIAL/ELSEWHERE-in-unit instead.
- **saq30a/saq30b (bond order of N₂/O₂) marked PARTIAL, not MATCHED**, against bank's laq2 (full MO-diagram treatment). The chemistry is a subset of laq2's answer, but laq2 is LAQ-depth and doesn't stand alone as a "what is meant by the term bond order + simple calculation" answer — treat this as a soft call.
- **saq18 (octet rule significance + limitations) marked PARTIAL rather than MISSING** because bank's vsaq1 does answer the "what is the rule" clause; the significance/limitations clause is fully absent, so this is a thin partial credit.
- **Bond-angle/length/enthalpy/order "notes" (vsaq7a, vsaq8d, laq36a–d) all marked PARTIAL** against bank's combined vsaq8/vsaq9 cards. The bank cards define the terms but weren't authored as the standalone expository "notes" the book's phrasing asks for.

It was NOT flagged as an ELSEWHERE match for laq36b, because chemistry-3 already has partial in-unit coverage (vsaq8) — ELSEWHERE is reserved here for topics with zero in-unit coverage.
