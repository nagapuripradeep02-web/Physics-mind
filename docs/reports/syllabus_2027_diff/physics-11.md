The bank files the identical stem under Oscillations, so it is reported as ELSEWHERE rather than MISSING; whether that's the "correct" home unit for this Thermal chapter is a call for the author/reviewer, not this diff.

Checked the Oscillations unit specifically for this stem/data (8.212 s, brass) — absent there too, so this is reported as MISSING, not ELSEWHERE.
