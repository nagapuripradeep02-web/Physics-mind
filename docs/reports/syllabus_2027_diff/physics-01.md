Treated as matched — the discovery/contribution IS the named effect/limit — but the card should be checked to confirm it actually names and explains the Raman effect / Chandrasekhar limit, not just biographical trivia.
- **vsaq5/vsaq13 → vsaq4** (`accuracy_vs_precision`): one "distinguish" bank card is used to cover both the book's plain "define" question and its "distinguish, with examples" question. Reasonable since distinguishing requires defining both terms first, but if the card is example-light it may under-serve vsaq13's "with suitable examples" requirement.
- **vsaq11 + vsaq16 → vsaq6** (`fundamental_vs_derived_units`): the book itself prints two near-duplicate questions (SI base vs. fundamental units, one vs. two examples). Both mapped to the same single bank card; flagging in case the book intends two distinct answers (unlikely, but the "SI base units" framing occasionally gets a different worked example set than "fundamental units").
- **vsaq8 → vsaq14** (`significant_figures_meaning`): the book's "why are they important" half is not independently verifiable from the bank's title text alone — only the full card body would confirm it addresses importance, not just the definition.
- **vsaq15 → vsaq20** (`principle_of_homogeneity`): same caveat — the book adds "how is it used to check whether an equation is correct", which the bank card's title doesn't confirm it covers.
- **saq1 → vsaq8** (`dimensional_analysis`, RE-CUT to SAQ): book also asks for "one application" — confirm the re-cut SAQ version adds an application, not just the bare definition.
