   restated text; unconfirmed whether the full authored answer names the scientist.

   brand-new card from scratch.

   book's LAQ derives **maximum velocity with friction** (θ and μ combined) while the bank's SAQ derives
   the **banking angle**, apparently frictionless. Classified MISSING because the derived quantity and
   assumptions differ; could not verify from the restated summary whether the bank card's full authored
   derivation already includes the friction term.
4. **prob12 (book) vs bank vsaq17 (lift_apparent_weight)**: the book's numeric two-case comparison (lift
   moving up at g/3 vs down at g/2, express the second in terms of the first apparent weight W) needs
   more than the bank card's general one-case formula. Classified MISSING for the problem, but vsaq17 is
   a direct building block — a card revision may be enough rather than an all-new PROBLEM card.
5. **saq9 (book) vs bank saq6 + saq10**: the book wants the three named friction TYPES (limiting,
   dynamic, rolling) explained; the bank has "laws of rolling friction" and "laws of static/kinetic
   friction" as separate cards, which overlap in subject matter but do not directly answer "explain the
   terms limiting/dynamic/rolling friction." Classified MISSING.
6. **saq1 (book) vs bank saq11 (types_of_inertia)**: the book wants TWO examples per kind of inertia; the
   bank card supplies only ONE per kind. Classified MATCHED (same core ask) but flagged as a depth gap
   rather than a clean match.
