## Notes

**Both named source defects are resolved, and resolved correctly.**

- *The "mm" rate.* `ts_ipe_m1b_ad_cone_water_height_rate_8m_6m` — the book prints "2 cubic metre/mm".
  The card reads the figure as **per minute**, authors the stem as "2 cubic metres per minute", carries
  m/min through to the box (dh/dt = 2/(9π) m/min ≈ 0.07), and records the change in `verification.note`
  instead of correcting silently. That is the only defensible reading: mm is not a unit of time, and the
  rest of the stem is in metres. Re-derived independently: r = 3h/4, V = 3πh³/16, dV/dt = (9π/16)h²·dh/dt,
  h = 4 → 9π·dh/dt = 2 → dh/dt = 2/(9π). Correct.
- *The bacteria count in seconds asked at "4 hours".* `ts_ipe_m1b_ad_bacteria_t_three_halves_rate` — the
  card authors the stem as **t = 4 seconds**, the only reading consistent with the count t³ᐟ² being in
  seconds and with the source's own substitution of 4 into the derivative, and states in
  `verification.note` that 4 hours would be t = 14400, a number the source never uses. dN/dt = (3/2)√t,
  at t = 4 → 3 bacteria per second. Correct, and the unit is right.
