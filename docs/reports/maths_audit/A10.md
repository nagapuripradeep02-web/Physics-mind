proves `tan70° − tan20° = 2 tan50°`, with the 2 arising correctly from tan20° = cot70° making the
denominator 1 + 1. Verified numerically: 2.747477 − 0.363970 = 2.383507 = 2 × 1.191754. Its twin
`ts_ipe_m1a_tr_vsaq_tan50_tan40` (2 tan10°) is likewise correct: 1.191754 − 0.839100 = 0.352654
= 2 × 0.176327. Neither card reproduces the book's printed `tan50°`.

only re-derived algebraically): sin²A + sin²B − sin²C at A=60°, B=70°, C=50° → 1.046198 on both
